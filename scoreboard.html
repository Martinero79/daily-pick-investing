Scoreboard — Ranked by Martinero Index

This is not a buy list. The Martinero Index is a fundamentals scorecard only — it measures growth, margins, returns on capital, balance sheet health, moat, valuation-vs-own-history and ownership at a fixed point in time. It takes no account of whether a stock is currently trending up or down, whether today's price is attractive, or whether a company will actually go on to succeed. The Momentum column (click either header to re-sort) is a separate, non-scored signal — it compares each company's most recent fiscal year revenue growth rate to the prior year, so a company accelerating from 10%→15% ranks above one decelerating from 20%→18% even though the latter's absolute growth is higher. It is not part of the Martinero Index and does not affect it. A high rank on either column is not a signal to buy, and a low rank is not a signal to avoid — verify everything yourself before acting on anything on this site.
