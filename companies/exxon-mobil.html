Exxon Mobil is the largest Western oil major, with a balance sheet that barely registers net debt (≈0.5x EBITDA), a 45-year record of annual dividend increases, and about $20B a year of share buybacks on top of a $17B dividend. What the scorecard doesn’t reward is the shape of the earnings: operating income of $35.4B in FY2025 is less than half the FY2022 peak of $68.0B, free cash flow has fallen 10% a year since FY2021 as capex more than doubled to $28.4B, and a 10.9% EBIT margin is thin next to asset-light compounders. The latest quarter (Q2 2026) shows how much of the picture is oil price: revenue jumped 44% year on year to $114.5B and net income doubled to $14.5B as crude spiked on the Gulf conflict, after a weak Q1 2026 ($4.2B). Valuation needs the same care — the trailing P/E of ≈23x looks far above the 12x FY21-25 average, but the denominator (TTM earnings) is not yet at the Q2 run-rate; the forward P/E of ≈12x is in line with history. What's Working

Scale and balance-sheet strength: net debt of only 0.47x EBITDA, a 45-year dividend-growth record, about $38B a year returned through dividends and buybacks, and a share count trending down (−3.9% in the last year). Exxon is a strong #2 by scale among global integrated oil & gas companies, ROIC (10.0%) clears even a conventional cost of capital, and Q2 2026 showed the earnings leverage the business has to higher oil prices (net income +105% YoY, $17.0B of quarterly FCF).

What to Watch

Cyclicality: Brent at about $114 (late Oct 2026, on Iran/Houthi–Saudi tensions) is the main driver of the recent upswing, and a de-escalation could reverse it quickly. FCF has fallen 10% a year since FY2021 as capex rose from $12.1B to $28.4B, and distributions currently exceed TTM free cash flow. Margins are structurally lower than asset-light peers (10.9% EBIT, down 6.1pp since FY2022), insider ownership is negligible, and at about 23x trailing earnings the stock trades well above its own FY21-25 average (a ratio of 1.87) even though the forward multiple (about 12x) looks ordinary. Q3 2026 results on Oct 30 are the next test.
