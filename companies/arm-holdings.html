Arm is the near-monopoly architecture licensor behind 99% of smartphone CPUs, now pushing into AI datacenter compute (Neoverse, CSS, and its first in-house chip, the Arm AGI CPU, due H2 2026). Q1 FY2027 delivered record revenue on accelerating royalty growth from AI infrastructure. The model's own numbers say: real growth and cash generation, but profitability hasn't caught up yet, ROIC still trails its unusually high cost of capital, and the business is majority-controlled by an external parent (SoftBank) rather than founder-aligned management.

What's working
Real, accelerating growth (royalties +22%, data-center royalties >2x); near-monopoly in mobile; net cash balance sheet; FCF conversion recovering; low capex relative to peers.
What to watch
ROIC still below its own cost of capital; RISC-V at 25% global processor share and growing ~30%/yr, including from Arm's own licensees (Qualcomm); SoftBank control with thin 13% float; margin volatility quarter to quarter; no real valuation history to anchor against.
