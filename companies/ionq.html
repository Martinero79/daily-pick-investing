What to Watch

Operating margin and ROIC are both deeply negative with no near-term path to the site's standard bars; EBIT and FCF losses have widened rather than narrowed over the window; capex intensity (12.6% of revenue) is well above an asset-light profile; and share count is up 102.9% on heavy equity-funded dilution, the common thread across all four names in this batch.
