The entire investment case rests on clinical and regulatory catalysts, not reported financials. This page tracks where HLP003 and HLP004 actually stand as of September 2026.
The path to a Q4 2026 readout, in plain terms

HLP003 (formerly CYB003), a proprietary deuterated psilocybin/psilocin analog, is Helus Pharma's lead asset for the adjunctive treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD) and holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation — the first such designation granted to an adjunctive neuroactive-steroid-class asset for MDD, per the company. It is in the Phase 3 PARADIGM program: APPROACH (n≈220, 16mg vs placebo, 2-dose regimen 3 weeks apart, primary endpoint MADRS change at 6 weeks) completed enrollment ahead of schedule in July 2026; EMBRACE (a second, larger pivotal study, n≈330, testing 16mg and 8mg vs placebo) was still enrolling as of the APPROACH completion announcement; and EXTEND is collecting long-term durability and safety data via participant rollover. Phase 2 data supporting the program showed improvement in depressive symptoms across all participants after two 16mg doses. Topline Phase 3 data from APPROACH and EMBRACE is guided for Q4 2026, with a targeted NDA filing in 2028 — as of this writing, no Phase 3 topline data has been released.

What's Working

HLP003 holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and completed Phase 3 APPROACH enrollment ahead of schedule in July 2026, with topline data (APPROACH and EMBRACE) guided for Q4 2026 — a genuinely near-term, defined catalyst rather than a multi-year wait. HLP004 already posted a statistically significant Phase 2 GAD signal (-10.4 points HAM-A, p<0.0001) in March 2026, giving the pipeline a second, earlier-stage shot on goal. The balance sheet carries no debt and a fresh $166.37M in net cash after the FY26 raise, automatically satisfying the net debt/EBITDA criterion, and capex is genuinely near-zero given the outsourced clinical-stage model. A new CEO with direct large-pharma-acquisition experience (Michael Halstead, ex-Intra-Cellular Therapies) was brought in specifically to manage the transition toward a potential launch, and co-founders Glavine and So have both been buying stock on the open market in recent weeks rather than selling.

What to Watch

This is a single-catalyst, binary bet: there is no revenue, no approved product, and no Phase 3 topline data yet — the entire near-term valuation rests on a readout that hasn't happened. Dilution is the standout negative on this scorecard: shares outstanding are up 103% in the past year and roughly 15.7x since FY22, and at the current ~$140.8M/yr burn rate the $166.37M cash balance implies only about 14 months of runway, meaning another raise (and further dilution) is a reasonable base case around or shortly after the Q4 2026 data, regardless of outcome. ROIC is deeply negative with no near-term path to profitability, and analyst price targets are unusually scattered ($16 to $62, consensus $40.40) — a sign of genuine disagreement about how to value a story this early and this binary. Compass Pathways and Definium Therapeutics remain ahead in the same race with Phase 3 data already in hand, so Helus is not a #1/#2 name today even with a credible near-term catalyst.
