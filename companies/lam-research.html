Lam Research is a California-based supplier of the machines that build chips: it makes etch tools (carving circuit patterns into silicon wafers), deposition tools (laying down thin films) and cleaning tools, and sells spares, upgrades and service on its large installed base. That second leg, “customer support & other”, was $8.3B or 36% of FY2026 revenue and is steadier than new-tool sales; the systems business ($14.9B) swings with memory (NAND and DRAM) and foundry/logic capital spending. FY2026 (to June 2026) was the best year on record: revenue $23.2B (+26.0%), GAAP operating income $8.2B (35.3% margin), EPS $5.76 (+38.8%, split-adjusted). The Sep-2026 quarter, reported Oct 21, set another record: revenue $8.22B (+54% YoY), EPS $2.20 on the company's non-GAAP basis ($2.19 GAAP), gross margin 52.4% and operating margin 40.2% (non-GAAP). Yet the shares closed at $221.31 on Nov 9, about 50% below the 52-week high of $438.50: they fell 11.8% on Oct 22, the day after the report, and 16.4% on Nov 5 (to $185.20, the day a $25B buyback was announced, amid a wider sell-off in chip-equipment stocks), before rebounding 8.2% and 10.4% on Nov 6 and Nov 9. Fundamentals are at a cyclical high and the price has de-rated hard, so the question for the next quarters is how much of the current demand is durable versus a peak in spending. This page takes no view on that.
Three distortions to keep in mind: (1) Cycle — revenue fell 14.5% in FY2024 and EBIT 18%, then rebounded; growth CAGRs and ratios depend heavily on which years bracket the window (FY2022 was itself a peak). (2) Fiscal calendar — FY ends late June, so “year” columns are Jul–Jun and quarter labels are fiscal (Q1 FY27 = Sep 2026); year-end share prices on the last page are late-December closes for readability. (3) 10:1 split (Oct 2024) — all per-share data and historical prices are restated; unadjusted quotes from before 2024 will look ten times higher.

What the quarter showed — and what the stock did

What's Working

The financial profile is excellent at the top line of the stack: 35.3% EBIT margin, ~79% ROIC against a ~14% estimated WACC, net cash, and gross margin up 5.4pp in three years. FY2026 and the Sep-2026 quarter were records (revenue +54% YoY in the latest quarter, free cash flow $3.24B). A large installed base generates 36% of revenue from services and upgrades, which cushions new-tool demand swings. Shares are down ~10% since FY2022, dividends have grown ~15% a year, and a $25B buyback was authorized. The stock trades at ~21.5x forward earnings versus a ~28.8x own-history average after a ~50% fall from its high.

What to Watch

This is a cyclical equipment maker at a record: revenue fell 14.5% in FY2024 and a 54% quarterly growth rate is the kind of figure that normalises. The stock has been very volatile (beta 1.85; -16.4% in one day on Nov 5) and reaction to a record report was negative, so the market is pricing something other than reported numbers (the cause is not verified here). FCF conversion is only ~60% on growth-year working capital, FCF fell in FY2026, and the trailing P/E (33x) is slightly above its own average. Capex is rising, customer concentration and China exposure are not quantified on this page, and several analyst targets have been cut. Market-share percentages, employee-plan ownership and next-quarter guidance could not be verified.
