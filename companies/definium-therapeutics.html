Definium Therapeutics is the renamed continuation of Mind Medicine (MindMed) Inc. — same Nasdaq-listed entity, ticker changed from MNMD to DFTX on January 15, 2026 alongside the corporate rebrand. It is a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company developing novel treatments for brain-health disorders, led by CEO Robert Barrow out of New York. The lead asset, DT120, is an orally-disintegrating tablet formulation of lysergide (LSD) in Phase 3 development for generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) and major depressive disorder (MDD). Through August-September 2026 the company reported positive Phase 3 topline results from three pivotal studies — Voyage and Panorama in GAD, and Emerge in MDD — and received FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation for DT120 in MDD; management has guided to an NDA filing in 2027. A second, earlier-stage asset, DT402 (the R-enantiomer of MDMA), is in Phase 2a for autism spectrum disorder. None of this shows up as revenue: Definium has never generated product sales in any fiscal year, so this dashboard's usual growth, margin and cash-conversion criteria mostly return N/A rather than a pass/fail verdict — the same treatment this site applies to other pre-revenue names like IonQ. What is measurable is a $353.79M TTM net loss (EPS -$3.48) funded by a balance sheet that swelled to $1.05B in net cash after large 2025-26 capital raises, a share count that has grown roughly 370% since FY2021 to fund those raises, and a stock up over 240% year-to-date purely on trial-data catalysts and analyst upgrades (18 analysts, consensus Strong Buy, average price target $71.65 — roughly 90% above the current price). Against the standard 14-criterion scorecard, only net debt/EBITDA (automatic on the net-cash position) and capex intensity (genuinely near-zero) are cleared outright; ROIC, share count trend and insider ownership all fail, and EBIT/FCF CAGR are scored partial since losses widened rather than narrowed. Result: 2 of 14 criteria met, Martinero Index 25/100. 2026 Phase 3 Data Readouts & Regulatory Path

Definium reported positive Phase 3 topline results from Voyage (its first Phase 3 study of DT120 ODT 100mcg in GAD) roughly five weeks before this writing, followed by Panorama (its second Phase 3 GAD study, topline announced Sep 14, 2026) and Emerge (Phase 3 in MDD, presented alongside Voyage data at Psych Congress 2026 on Sep 8, 2026). On Aug 27, 2026 the FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy designation to DT120 ODT in MDD following the positive Phase 3 results. Management has said regulatory discussions are underway on a potential dual-indication (GAD + MDD) NDA filing, targeted for 2027. Open-label retreatment data (Part B of the pivotal program) showed 60-70% mild-symptom/remission rates on intermittent redosing, per the company's Sep 2026 TD Cowen conference transcript. DT402 (R-MDMA), the second pipeline asset, remains in Phase 2a for autism spectrum disorder with no data readout guided yet.

What's Working

Three positive Phase 3 topline readouts in 2026 (Voyage and Panorama in GAD, Emerge in MDD) plus FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation de-risk the clinical and regulatory path for DT120 considerably relative to where this program stood a year ago, and a clear NDA-filing timeline (2027) gives the story a defined near-term catalyst. The balance sheet is a genuine strength: $1.05B in net cash against just $36.47M of debt gives several years of runway at the current burn rate, automatically satisfying the net debt/EBITDA criterion, and capex intensity is genuinely near-zero given the outsourced-manufacturing clinical-stage model. Analyst sentiment is strongly positive (18 analysts, consensus Strong Buy, average target $71.65, roughly 90% above the current price), with recent target hikes from BofA, Canaccord, Baird, Oppenheimer and RBC following the trial data.

What to Watch

Every financial criterion on this scorecard that can be computed at all is either failing (ROIC, share count, insider ownership) or scored partial on widening losses (EBIT/FCF CAGR) — seven of fourteen criteria are structurally N/A because there is simply no revenue or approved product yet. The entire investment case now hinges on regulatory execution (a clean NDA filing and FDA review of a first-in-class oral LSD therapeutic, an unprecedented approval pathway with no direct precedent to benchmark against) and, if approved, a commercial launch this dashboard cannot yet score. Shares outstanding are up roughly 370% since FY2021 and further dilution is likely ahead of or around any commercial launch; insider ownership at 2.66% is modest; and the stock's 240%+ year-to-date run on binary trial-data catalysts means it is now priced for continued clinical/regulatory success — a single unfavorable FDA interaction or safety signal could reverse the re-rating quickly. Beta of 2.24 and a 52-week range of $9.35-$49.70 underline how volatile and event-driven this name already is.
