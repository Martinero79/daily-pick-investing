Amphenol is a Connecticut-based maker of the connectors, cables and sensors that carry power and signals inside almost every electronic system: servers and switches, cars, aircraft, industrial machinery and phones. It was founded in 1932, runs a decentralised model of many small business units and grows by buying specialist interconnect companies. The recent story is AI: demand for high-speed interconnect and power products in data centres has lifted Communications Solutions from $4.8B in FY2021 to $12.1B in FY2025, and $6.6B in Q3 2026 alone. Group revenue of $10.2B in Q3 2026 was +65% YoY with a 29.7% GAAP operating margin; the company guides Q4 to $10.3-10.5B of sales and adjusted EPS of $0.81-0.83, and FY2026 to $36.9-37.1B and $2.82-2.84. Part of the growth is acquired — the Q2 call summary names CommScope — and net debt has jumped from $4.6B to $12.6B, though still ~1.2x EBITDA. The stock closed Oct 30 at $79.30, about 12% below its 52-week high, after rising on the Q3 report and then falling 3.4% on Friday; the trailing GAAP P/E is ~34x.
Three distortions to keep in mind: (1) Stock splits — all share prices and per-share numbers are split-adjusted (2-for-1 on Sep 3, 2026, plus an earlier split), so older quoted prices and EPS differ. (2) M&A — acquisitions, notably CommScope's business, contribute to 2026 growth; the size and closing date of the deal were not verified here and no organic-versus-acquired bridge is shown (management cites strong organic growth on the Q2 call). (3) GAAP noise — Q1 2026 GAAP net income ($933M, EPS $0.36) sits well below the quarters around it; the S&P data does not say why (acquisition-related costs are a likely, unverified cause), and it lowers the trailing GAAP EPS used for the P/E.

What's Working

Growth, margins and returns are all at the top end of the site's bands: revenue +20.7% a year, EBIT +29.2% and FCF +38.8% over four years, GAAP operating margin of 26-30%, gross margin up 5.3pp in three years, and ROIC of ~20% against an estimated ~10% cost of capital. Demand is broad (datacom, industrial, defence, automotive), Q3 2026 beat the company's own guide by roughly $0.8-0.9B and guidance for Q4 implies another ~60% YoY. Leverage is still modest at ~1.2x despite the acquisitions, capex is only ~4% of revenue and the dividend is covered about four times.

What to Watch

The stock is priced for continued hypergrowth: ~34x trailing and ~27x forward earnings, above its own trailing average, and an AI-capex slowdown would hit Communications Solutions (64% of sales) hard. Part of 2026 growth is acquired, net debt has nearly tripled in nine months and ROIC will be tested as acquisitions are absorbed. FCF conversion is only 72% of EBIT (working capital and capex growing with sales). The market is fragmented and Amphenol has no founder or insider block. Unverified: market-share ranking, the acquisitions' size and contribution, the cause of the weak Q1 2026 GAAP quarter and the exact current share count.
