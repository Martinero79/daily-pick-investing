What's Working

This is a company steadily converging on GAAP profitability rather than one still figuring out its cost structure — five straight years of narrowing losses, non-GAAP operating margin at an all-time high, and free cash flow that's already strong and growing (51% CAGR). Founder and CEO Jay Chaudhry's family holds roughly 35% of the company, the strongest insider alignment anywhere in this batch, and the stock's -32.5% year is a genuine disconnect from the underlying trend rather than a reaction to it — the current P/FCF sits at less than half its own historical range.

What to Watch

Zscaler still hasn't crossed into GAAP operating profitability, and the competitive field in SASE/SSE is genuinely getting tighter — Palo Alto Networks' SASE ARR is now growing faster than Zscaler's, and Netskope adds a third serious contender. Share count is up 17% over five years from stock-based comp, and management's own FY2027 ARR growth guidance (16-17%) signals a deliberate deceleration from current rates, worth weighing against whatever growth premium the market re-attaches to the stock.
