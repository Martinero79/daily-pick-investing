What's Working

Revenue has compounded at +34.9%/yr for four straight years without a single sub-21% year — the most consistent growth story in this batch. FCF has grown even faster in dollar terms in FY26 and holds a steady 27-32% margin every year, funding a genuinely debt-free, $4.41B-net-cash balance sheet. Q2 FY2027 delivered record net-new ARR (+51% YoY) and a guidance raise, and CrowdStrike holds a clear Leader position in the category per Gartner's own rankings.

What to Watch

GAAP operating income has never been positive in five fiscal years — heavy stock-based compensation is a real, structural cost of this business, not a one-off, and it's driving both the negative GAAP margins and the ~12% share dilution since FY2022. The current 145x P/FCF is roughly double the company's own 5-year average, mostly reflecting the size of the Aug 27, 2026 earnings pop rather than a slow re-rating — a name to watch for a better entry point rather than chase into. CEO Kurtz's ownership stake has fallen under 1% alongside an active selling plan, which is worth monitoring for what it signals about insider conviction at current prices.
