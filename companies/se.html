Sea Limited's story is a genuine, multi-year turnaround rather than a straightforward growth or value case. Shopee has grown into the clear #1 e-commerce platform across Southeast Asia (roughly 52-53% of platform GMV, well ahead of TikTok Shop and Lazada), Garena's Free Fire remains a top mobile game franchise, and Monee (formerly SeaMoney) has scaled its consumer credit book to $11.1 billion. For years all three ran at a loss while Sea burned cash to win share; FY2021 group operating margin was -15.90%. That has now genuinely reversed: TTM operating margin is 8.24%, free cash flow has compounded from -$564M (FY2021) to $4.51B (FY2025), and revenue growth has actually re-accelerated to +43% TTM even at a much larger base. The catch is that GAAP profitability, while real, is still thin relative to the cash generation, share count keeps drifting upward from stock-based comp, and Southeast Asian e-commerce is a genuinely three-way contested market with TikTok Shop growing 40-55% a year and closing the gap. Sea is also pushing Shopee into Brazil and the rest of Latin America with a new "Turbo Delivery" service explicitly aimed at Amazon and MercadoLibre — a direct overlap with two other names in this dashboard batch.

What's Working

This is a genuine, multi-year turnaround, not a story still waiting to happen: revenue growth has re-accelerated even at a much larger base, operating margin has climbed for five straight years, free cash flow has compounded to $4.51B, and Shopee, Garena and Monee are all now GAAP operating-profitable at the same time for the first time in Sea's history. Balance sheet stayed net-cash throughout, and ROIC comfortably clears both the 15% bar and Sea's own cost of capital.

What to Watch

Operating margin is still thin relative to GAAP standards and relative to Sea's own FCF margin, and TikTok Shop is a genuinely fast-growing competitive threat in Sea's core Southeast Asia market. Share count keeps drifting upward from stock-based comp, and the current valuation sits slightly above Sea's own recent trading history rather than offering a clear discount. The new Latin America expansion is a real opportunity but also a fresh, unproven front against entrenched incumbents Amazon and MercadoLibre.
