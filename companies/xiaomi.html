Xiaomi is a smartphone, consumer-IoT and internet-services group that launched an electric-vehicle business in 2024 (SU7, then YU7). The EV bet drove the last two years of growth: revenue rose 35.0% in FY2024 and 25.0% in FY2025 to ¥457.3B, EV-and-other revenue reached ¥106.1B in 2025 (411,000 vehicles delivered), and the segment posted its first full-year operating profit. But the five-year view is far less dramatic: revenue CAGR from FY2021 is only 8.6% because FY2022-23 were down years, and the core is weakening in 2026. Smartphone shipments fell 26% YoY in Q2 2026 (to 31.2M units, still #3 globally at ≈11.5% share) as memory-chip costs surged, group gross margin slipped to 19.8%, and the EV segment swung back to a quarterly operating loss (¥3.1B in Q1, ¥2.6B in Q2 2026) as a new SU7 generation ramped. The share price has fallen by more than half from its mid-2025 peak, to HK$23.88. What's Working

Net cash of roughly ¥77B, a founder who owns about 23% of the shares and controls about 64% of the votes, low capex intensity (≈2.8% of revenue), gross margin up about 5pp over three years, and a trailing P/E (≈16.8x) below its own year-end average. The EV segment turned a full-year operating profit in 2025, deliveries are growing (+28% YoY in Q2 2026; 2026 target 550k), and Xiaomi is building in-house chips and an overseas push that could diversify it away from handsets.

What to Watch

Consolidated EBIT margin (7.1%) is far below the 20% bar and the TTM figure has already dropped to 4.8%. Q2 2026 adjusted net profit fell 42.6% YoY, smartphone shipments fell 26% on memory-cost pressure, IoT revenue fell 19%, and the EV segment is back to a ¥2.6B quarterly loss (Q1: ¥3.1B) amid an intense China price war and falling domestic car sales. TTM free cash flow is negative, the share count has risen about 5% over five years, and the stock is down about 60% from its mid-2025 high. IFRS earnings include volatile investment gains, so the headline P/E overstates how cheap the shares are on operating earnings, and the forward P/E is higher than trailing. India regulatory scrutiny is an additional unquantified risk.
