The catch is timing. What's Working

This is a genuinely, durably GAAP-profitable cybersecurity platform — operating margins have expanded every year since FY2023, revenue growth just accelerated to +31% YoY on AI-security demand, and Next-Gen Security ARR is compounding at 60% YoY. The balance sheet remains net cash even after a $25bn acquisition, and CEO Nikesh Arora has recently been buying shares in the open market rather than selling. CyberArk adds a genuine #1-or-#2 identity security position on top of the existing network/cloud security leadership.

What to Watch

Valuation is the clear risk here: the current 79.8x P/FCF is roughly 2.6x the company's own five-year average, mostly reflecting a stock price that's tripled since April 2026 rather than a slow re-rating. Share count is up 35% over five years, and the most recent quarter's GAAP operating loss (even though one-off) is a reminder that CyberArk integration costs will keep showing up for a few more quarters. FY2026 full-year results, due Sep 1, 2026, should clarify how quickly margins normalize post-deal.
