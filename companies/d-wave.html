What's Working

Essentially the only commercial-scale public vendor of quantum annealing hardware — a genuinely distinct, more concentrated architecture niche than the broader gate-model race, giving D-Wave a credible #1 claim within its own specific sub-category. Net debt/EBITDA is automatically Met on the strength of $841M in net cash. Gross margin expanded 10.5pp over FY2021-FY2025 (72.1% → 82.6%), the only one of the four names with genuine margin-stability strength.

What to Watch

Operating margin and ROIC are both deeply negative with no near-term path to the site's standard bars; EBIT and FCF losses have widened rather than narrowed over the window; capex intensity (15.7% of revenue) is well above an asset-light profile; and share count is up 216.6% on heavy equity-funded dilution, the common thread across all four names in this batch.
