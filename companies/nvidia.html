What's Working

Every growth, profitability, ROIC, balance-sheet and valuation criterion clears the bar; trades cheaper than its own 5-year average multiples despite the AI narrative; net cash growing fast; buybacks shrinking share count; still the clear #1 in AI accelerators.

What to Watch

FCF conversion below 80% on inventory/receivables build — watch if this becomes structural; AMD and custom ASICs (Broadcom, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, Microsoft Maia) are funded and shipping, with ASIC shipment growth (+44.6% YoY) now outpacing merchant GPU growth (+16.1%); thin insider ownership; concentration risk if you already hold Arm, Broadcom or other AI-infrastructure names — same cycle, different altitude.
