Compass Pathways is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing COMP360, a proprietary synthetic formulation of psilocybin, for treatment-resistant depression (TRD). Both pivotal Phase 3 trials — COMP005 (single-dose) and COMP006 (repeat-dose, ~600 patients) — hit their primary endpoints with statistically significant, clinically meaningful and durable reductions in depression severity (MADRS) versus control, most recently confirmed by 26-52 week durability data reported through September 2026. COMP360 holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and UK ILAP designation, and the FDA has granted a rolling NDA review plus a Commissioner's National Priority Voucher slot (a shortened 1-2 month review window after formal filing) — final NDA submission is targeted for Q4 2026, with a potential U.S. commercial launch in H1 2027. Latest Headlines (Jul-Sep 2026)

Both pivotal Phase 3 trials have now read out positive: COMP005 (single 25mg dose vs. placebo) hit its primary endpoint with a -3.6 point MADRS treatment difference (p<0.001), with 52-week open-label (Part C) data reported Sep 2026 showing added benefit from a further dose and durability out to one year. COMP006 (repeat-dose, ~600 patients, a more chronic/severe TRD population) hit its primary endpoint with a -3.8 point MADRS difference for 25mg vs. 1mg (p<0.001), with positive 26-week Part B durability data reported Jul 7, 2026. Across both trials, COMP360 has shown a generally well-tolerated safety profile, with most treatment-emergent adverse events mild-to-moderate and resolving within 24 hours. The FDA has granted a rolling NDA review and selected COMP360 for its Commissioner's National Priority Voucher pilot (a 1-2 month review window after formal filing); management has guided to completing the NDA submission in Q4 2026 and a potential U.S. commercial launch in H1 2027, alongside an ongoing Phase 2 program in PTSD.
