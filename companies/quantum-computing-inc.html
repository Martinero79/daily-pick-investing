Latest Quarter Headlines

Q2 2026 revenue was $5.6M, up from $61K a year earlier and $3.7M in Q1 2026, driven mainly by photonics product sales and the newly integrated NHanced Semiconductors foundry (QUBT's third acquisition of 2026, a $73.1M cash/stock deal for US-based advanced-packaging capacity, plus up to $72M in earn-outs). Operating expenses rose to $21.8M (from $10.2M) partly on $7.3M of acquisition costs. Net loss narrowed to $11.8M from $36.5M a year earlier. The company ended the quarter with $1.3B in cash and investments against total liabilities of just $47.2M.
