What's Working

Nearly every metric on this page is best-in-class: revenue and margin both accelerating for five straight quarters, a genuine ROIC/WACC spread of over 44 points, and a rock-solid balance sheet. The #1 HBM position is the single most important fact here — it's the product Nvidia and other AI accelerator makers cannot ship without.

What to Watch

Memory is a famously cyclical, capital-intensive business — FY2023's loss year is a reminder this can turn quickly if AI capex slows or HBM oversupply emerges (Micron and Samsung are both investing hard to close the gap). FCF conversion, while improving, still lags the 80% bar, and capex intensity remains structurally high. Valuation is also genuinely stretched once you normalize for the one-off gain propping up the headline P/E — roughly 2.2x the stock's own history, not a discount. A single quarter of AI-demand disappointment could move this scorecard meaningfully.
