The path to Phase 3, in plain terms

GH Research's Phase 2b trial of GH001 in treatment-resistant depression met its primary endpoint with a highly significant placebo-adjusted MADRS reduction of -15.5 points at Day 8 (p<0.0001); the open-label extension showed a 73% remission rate at six months with infrequent visits and no mandated psychotherapy add-on. Full results were published in JAMA Psychiatry in 2026. The FDA placed GH001's US IND on clinical hold at one point, then lifted it in January 2026, clearing the way for US enrollment and a global Phase 3 pivotal program. Since then, GH Research has completed the two remaining Phase 1 studies needed to finalize the program: GH001-HV-106 (its proprietary aerosol delivery device, in healthy volunteers) and GH001-HV-109 (the US IND-opening clinical pharmacology study) — both completed and reported in the Q2 2026 update, with doses selected for the global Phase 3 program. As of this writing, Phase 3 enrollment has not yet been confirmed as started and no Phase 3 topline data exists — the pivotal readout remains a forward catalyst, not a reported result.

What to Watch

This is a single-asset, binary-catalyst story: there is no revenue, no approved product, and no Phase 3 topline data yet — the entire valuation rests on a pivotal readout that hasn't started reading out. GH Research is viewed as smaller and earlier-stage than Compass Pathways and Definium Therapeutics in the same space, so it isn't a #1/#2 name today. Share count is already up 12.74% in a year from equity dilution, and more raises are a reasonable base case as Phase 3 costs come due — cash covers a multi-year runway at the current burn rate (roughly $50-70M/year operating loss) but not the full cost of a global pivotal program plus a potential launch.
