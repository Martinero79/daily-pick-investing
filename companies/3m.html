3M is a 124-year-old materials-science conglomerate. Since it spun off its Health Care business as Solventum on April 1, 2024 it has been a smaller company: about $25B of revenue, growing low single digits, with GAAP operating margin of 18.9% in FY2025 and 23.7% in Q3 2026. The reported history is hard to read. The Solventum spin removed roughly a quarter of the old revenue, 2023 contains the very large PFAS public-water-supplier and Combat Arms earplug settlement charges (GAAP operating loss of $10.2B), and PFAS-related payments, divestitures and the ups and downs of the retained Solventum stake keep moving GAAP earnings and cash flow. Q3 2026 was the best-received report in a while: adjusted EPS $2.50 against $2.38 expected, guidance raised, and an expanded optical-connectivity relationship with Amazon for data centers, which lifted the stock 5.8% on the day. The stock closed at $174.34 on Oct 30, about 26x trailing GAAP EPS and about 19x the adjusted 2026 EPS guide.
How to read the numbers on this page: (1) FY22-FY25 are continuing operations (Solventum shown as discontinued), as restated by S&P Global from 3M's filings; FY21 is the originally reported pre-spin figure including Health Care and is excluded from all CAGRs. (2) All financials are GAAP; 3M's “adjusted” numbers (which exclude PFAS/litigation costs, amortization from Q3 2026, transformation costs and the Solventum stake revaluation) are quoted only when labelled. (3) Cash-flow figures include discontinued operations until the spin and are net of settlement payments. Q3 2026 GAAP EPS of $2.63 includes a $0.60 per-share gain from the Solventum stake.

Quarterly Development — Q3 2026: Beat, Raise and an Amazon Deal
Latest reported quarter: Q3 2026 (three months to Sep 30, 2026), reported Tue Oct 20, 2026 before the open. GAAP sales $6.58B, +1.0% YoY; GAAP operating margin 23.7% (+150bp); GAAP EPS $2.63 (+70%); adjusted EPS $2.50 (+12%) on 3M's new basis.
What moved the stock: raised guidance and the data-center angle

The numbers. GAAP organic growth was only 0.2%; adjusted organic growth (which excludes manufactured PFAS products 3M is exiting) was 3.3%. Segments: Safety & Industrial sales $3.18B (+9.1%), Transportation & Electronics $2.00B (+0.4%), Consumer $1.31B (-0.4%), Other $0.10B (versus $0.30B a year ago as PFAS products wind down). Adjusted operating margin was 26.2% (+110bp); adjusted EPS $2.50 compared with $2.38 expected. GAAP EPS of $2.63 is higher than adjusted because it includes a $0.60 per-share gain from the Solventum stake, offset by $0.19 of litigation/PFAS costs, $0.08 transformation costs and $0.20 of amortization and acquisition costs.

The news flow. Before the print (Oct 19) 3M announced an expanded commercial relationship with Amazon for optical-connectivity and other data-center products, including a warrant for Amazon to buy up to 3.2M 3M shares tied to $6B of cumulative sales over seven years and other milestones, and a Taiwan collaboration with GSEO in which 3M takes about 4.7%. 2026 adjusted EPS guidance was raised to $9.10-9.15 (from $8.80-8.95; the new range also reflects the change to exclude amortization). The stock rose 5.8% on Oct 20 to $171.37 and was $174.34 on Oct 30.

Caveats. The data-center revenue has yet to show up in reported numbers; the warrant is an equity cost attached to it. GAAP free cash flow was only $230M in Q3 (operating cash flow $486M less $256M capex) versus the $1.2B “adjusted free cash flow” 3M reported, which as we read the release excludes settlement payments. Q4 2025's 33.6% gross margin and 12.4% operating margin show how special items can swing a single quarter.

What's Working

Operating momentum: Q3 2026 GAAP operating margin of 23.7% and adjusted 26.2% (+110bp), adjusted organic growth of 3.3% with Safety & Industrial up 9%, and guidance raised. Net debt is down from $13.6B in 2021 to $8.2B (1.3x EBITDA), capex is light (3.7% of sales), and the share count is down about 10% since 2021. The Amazon/data-center optical-connectivity relationship and new GSEO collaboration give a new growth story.

What to Watch

Reported growth is near zero (GAAP organic +0.2% in Q3; FY22-25 revenue CAGR -1.6%) and GAAP FCF is a fraction of the adjusted FCF 3M guides to, because settlement payments for PFAS and earplug cases continue. Ongoing PFAS litigation (including a roughly $1.4B Australian government claim) is an open-ended risk, and GAAP earnings still swing on stake revaluations and special items. Transportation & Electronics and Consumer are flat. The trailing GAAP multiple (about 26x) is above its own history on distorted earnings. ROIC, market share and the criterion-7 adjustment are estimates or judgment.
