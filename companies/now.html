ServiceNow is the most complete "core enterprise OS" growth story on this site so far: it commands roughly 44% of the global ITSM market (more than 2.5x its nearest rival), and unlike most mature SaaS names, its growth has been accelerating rather than decelerating — Q2 2026 revenue grew 24.0% YoY, the fastest quarter in over two years, with management now targeting the "Rule of 60" (revenue growth + non-GAAP operating margin). The AI story is real and quantified: AI-related annual contract value crossed $1 billion for the first time in Q2 2026, agentic AI deployments are up ninefold in nine months, and remaining performance obligations reached $29B on longer customer commitments. CEO Bill McDermott has leaned hard into the "fastest-growing major enterprise software and cybersecurity company" framing, backed by the April 2026 acquisition of Armis Security ($7.75B) to build out an AI-native cybersecurity/asset-visibility platform, financed initially by a term loan and refinanced via a $4.0B bond sale in May 2026 that drew nearly 10x oversubscription. That acquisition is the main reason the balance sheet flipped from net cash to a modest net debt position — but net debt/EBITDA of 0.60x remains comfortably low. The scorecard's soft spots are GAAP operating margin (still below this framework's 20% bar, and compressed sharply in the most recent quarter on higher SG&A/R&D spend even as non-GAAP margin expanded), ROIC not yet clearing 15%, and thin insider/founder ownership (0.14%) reflecting a professional-manager-led company rather than a founder-controlled one. The stock itself tells a starkly different story than the fundamentals: down 18.5% over the past year even as growth accelerated, though it has rallied hard over the past six weeks alongside a broader software-sector recovery.

Q2 2026 Headlines

Total revenue reached $3.987B, up 24.0% YoY — the fastest growth in over two years and well ahead of the mid-teens growth typical of mature $10B+ SaaS companies. Subscription revenue was $3.877B, up 24.5% YoY. Non-GAAP operating margin came in at 29.5%, 300bps above guidance, while GAAP operating margin compressed sharply to 4.06% (from 13.34% the prior quarter) on a roughly $511M sequential jump in SG&A and R&D expense — consistent with elevated stock-based compensation and integration costs tied to the Armis acquisition, not a deterioration in the underlying business. AI products crossed $1 billion in annual contract value for the first time, a threshold CEO Bill McDermott had targeted for over a year; agentic AI deployments grew ninefold in nine months, and first-time agentic AI buyers rose 45% YoY. Management raised full-year subscription revenue guidance to $15.755-15.770B (21% constant-currency growth) and non-GAAP operating margin guidance to 31.5%. Remaining performance obligations reached $29B, and the company describes itself as operating at the "Rule of 56," tracking toward "Rule of 60."

What's Working

ServiceNow has the strongest growth durability of any core-enterprise-SaaS name covered on this site — 20%+ revenue growth sustained (and now accelerating) for five straight years off an already-large base, a dominant #1 ITSM market position with real distance from its nearest competitor, and a genuinely fast-scaling AI product line (Now Assist/agentic AI) that crossed $1B in ACV this quarter. The balance sheet remains conservatively levered even after a large strategic acquisition, and the stock trades meaningfully cheaper than its own recent history despite the growth acceleration.

What to Watch

GAAP operating margin is still below this framework's 20% bar and just had an unusually sharp one-quarter compression tied to Armis integration and stock-based compensation — worth confirming this normalizes next quarter rather than becoming a trend. ROIC hasn't cleared 15% despite strong growth, and insider/founder ownership is the thinnest of any name on this site, reflecting professional (not founder) leadership. The stock's -18.5% 52-week decline despite accelerating fundamentals is itself a signal worth understanding before treating the current valuation as a clean discount.
